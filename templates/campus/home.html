The steps and the dates

  1. Assess your students on the theme Thirukkural Payilvom – Thirukkural Abhyas Karen, by the online assessment below or by any other suitable mode, on or before {{ period[1].strftime('%d %B %Y') if period[1] else 'the date given by CICT' }}. The study material in 22 languages and English is in the repository and the Thirukkural browser.
  2. Select one student as the winner, on merit, and nominate them with one Faculty Supervisor/Guide, endorsed by the Director/Registrar/Principal/Head of the Institution (nomination form).
  3. Register the selected student on this portal with the personal link that the portal sends when you select the student below, on or before {{ reg_end.strftime('%d %B %Y') if reg_end else 'the date given by CICT' }}: photograph, ID proof, the signed nomination form and the Faculty Supervisor/Guide.
  4. CICT consolidates the selected students; the selected students receive a confirmation, the study material, the online orientation and a stipend of ₹10,000, write a 4–5 page research paper and deliver a one-hour presentation in your institution. Your institution organises campus activities on the theme and reports to the Nodal Institution.
