The accounts from a spreadsheet

  1. Download the sheet (Excel): every State/UT with its Nodal Institution, and the accounts that exist.
  2. Fill in the name, e-mail and phone of each Nodal Officer. One officer may take several States/UTs: the same e-mail on several rows makes one account. Rows left empty are left alone.
  3. Upload it. The portal first shows what it will do; nothing is created before you confirm. Each new account is mailed a temporary password.
