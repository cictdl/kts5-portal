Payment list

Only entries that have been approved against their proof, of students in the selected list; a payment made before a student left the list stays in the list of paid entries. Every export of 'Approved, not yet paid' is a numbered payment list, to be marked as paid below once the bank has carried it out. Account numbers are written as text. Every export is written to the audit log.

Mark a payment list as paid

After the bank has carried out the transfers of a payment list (an export of 'Approved, not yet paid'): one reference (the batch or the sanction order) and the date for the entries of that list, which are marked paid at the amount the list went out with. Entries approved after it, or changed since, are left out and named. A list that never went to the bank is discarded: nothing is marked paid and its entries go into the next list.
