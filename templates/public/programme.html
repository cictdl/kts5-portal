Dedicated portal

This website: a single place for programme administration, participant registration, content delivery and coordination among the participating agencies. A ten-part academic video lecture series on the Thirukkural, translated, dubbed or subtitled into the 21 scheduled languages other than Tamil, delivered language by language with a 20-minute live question-and-answer segment in each session.

6

One-month internship

ஒரு மாதப் பயிற்சி · एक माह की इंटर्नशिप

One hundred of the selected students receive a one-month internship, with a stipend of ₹10,000 each, to deepen their understanding of the Thirukkural, its values, philosophy and contemporary relevance.

7

Student presentation and knowledge dissemination

மாணவர் உரை · विद्यार्थी प्रस्तुति

Every delegate is equipped to deliver a one-hour presentation or lecture on the Thirukkural in their own college under the guidance of a designated faculty mentor, so that each of the 1,000 campuses hears the couplets from one of its own.

II Apps, videos, films, conferences and public displays

Thirukkural Multilingual App

All 1,330 couplets with CICT's translations into the 22 scheduled languages, two Tamil commentaries, English prose, word-level grammar, metrical analysis and palm-leaf references. Offline, ad-free, CC BY 4.0.

Educational video series

Twenty-minute lectures on selected couplets in Indian languages, explaining their meaning, values and relevance, published on CICT's YouTube channels and this portal.

Thirukkural audiobooks

Recitations of the couplets with meanings, explanations and translations in multiple languages, designed for visually impaired students, auditory learners and non-Tamil audiences.

Thirukkural Recitation App

Verse-by-verse recitation of the entire text with Dr Mu. Varadarajan's Tamil commentary, for correct pronunciation and study of the three sections.

Short films

Fifteen-minute films dramatising selected couplets on ethical leadership, social harmony, truthfulness, gratitude and civic duty, screened at KTS venues, campuses and broadcasters.

Reels and shorts

Sixty- to ninety-second AI-assisted animated videos on individual couplets with multilingual subtitles, published daily on CICT's social channels.

Annual National Conference on the Thirukkural

Held in major cities with the Bharatiya Bhasha Samiti and Banaras Hindu University, bringing scholars and translators from every language region together.

Public displays

Couplets with translations on passenger information systems and signage at railway stations, airports and highway toll plazas, in coordination with Indian Railways, AAI and NHAI.

Comic books

Illustrated stories inspired by the Thirukkural for children, first in English and Hindi, distributed through school boards and downloadable from this portal.

Posters and charts

Print-ready multilingual posters for schools, colleges and libraries, distributed to CBSE, KVS, JNV and state networks and available for download.

III Reaching non-Tamil-speaking students

Daily Kural

A 200-day calendar of couplets, one for each morning assembly, with translation and meaning in the school's language, distributed as printed flip-charts and digital packages.

Storytelling

Script banks, narration guides and theatrical prompts so that students present the values of the Thirukkural through stories, speeches, skits, plays and dance-dramas.

Thirukkural Gaming App

Crosswords, puzzles, complete-the-line, memory pairs, Wordle-style and typing challenges with localised interfaces and beginner-to-scholar tiers.
