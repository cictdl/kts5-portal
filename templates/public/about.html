Kashi Tamil Sangamam celebrates the civilisational bond between Tamil Nadu and Kashi. It was conceived as an expression of Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat: two of India's oldest centres of learning, Kashi on the Ganga and the Tamil country on the Vaigai and the Kaveri, meeting to rediscover shared traditions of language, scholarship, faith, craft and cuisine.

The fifth edition: Thirukkural Payilvom

KTS 5.0 takes the Thirukkural, the 1,330 couplets of Thiruvalluvar, as its unifying theme. The couplets speak of virtue, wealth and love in a language that every Indian tradition recognises as its own, which is why the text has been translated into all twenty-two languages of the Eighth Schedule by the Central Institute of Classical Tamil.

Under the theme திருக்குறள் பயில்வோம் · तिरुक्कुरल अभ्यास करें, one thousand students from one thousand colleges across India will register, take an online test, study the Thirukkural through a ten-lecture orientation in their own language, and then carry the text back to their campuses through student-led presentations.

Who does what

  • Ministry of Education is the nodal ministry for the Sangamam.
  • Central Institute of Classical Tamil, Chennai implements KTS 5.0: this portal, registration and selection, the multilingual orientation, the digital repository, apps, films, publications and school outreach.
  • IIT Madras and Banaras Hindu University are the nodal and coordinating institutes on the Tamil Nadu and Kashi sides.
  • Indian Railways, the Airports Authority of India, NHAI, the Ministry of Culture, UGC, AICTE, CBSE, KVS and the state governments of Tamil Nadu and Uttar Pradesh partner for displays, outreach, travel and venues. See {{ t('nav.partners') }}.
